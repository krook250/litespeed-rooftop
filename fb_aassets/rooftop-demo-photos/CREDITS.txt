ROOFTOP DEMO PHOTOS — attribution
=================================
Every image here is from Wikimedia Commons under public domain, CC0, CC BY or
CC BY-SA. CC BY and CC BY-SA REQUIRE visible credit wherever the image is
published. site/demo/credits.html already carries these for the marketing demo;
if these photos go on app.rooftopauto.com storefronts, the credit has to appear
there too.

Folders are named <shots>shot-<make>-<model>-<colour>. Shot count matters:
Meta needs 1 image to list an item and 2 to reach Marketplace.
Colour is read off the actual photo — set the vehicle's colour to match.
