Questions

The ones every dealer asks.

Do I have to leave my DMS?

No. Rooftop sits next to it. Your DMS keeps the books, the deals, the titles and the floorplan. Rooftop handles the inventory record that goes out to the world — photos, merchandising, your website and every channel. Nothing gets ripped out.

What happens to my current website?

Rooftop builds you one and points your domain at it. Same address, same branding, and the inventory on it updates the instant you change a record instead of whenever your current provider gets around to it. If you would rather keep the site you have, we can feed it instead — tell us who built it.

How fast does a price change actually reach each channel?

Your own site is instant. Meta usually re-crawls the catalog inside fifteen minutes. Google Vehicle Ads re-fetches the feed up to four times a day. Cars.com and Autotrader pull overnight, so an afternoon change is live by morning. We show you the real clock on every one rather than a checkmark that only means we sent it.

What if a listing gets rejected?

It shows up as a rejection with the actual reason — image too small, missing a required attribute, unmapped trim — on the unit and in your feed. A tool that catches a broken listing is worth more than one that pretends listings never break.

Can you bring my current inventory over?

Yes, and we do it, not you. Most lots are live in a few days. If your photos are already online we pull those too.

I run more than one lot. Does that cost double?

First rooftop is $149 a month, each additional rooftop is $99. You can run them as separate storefronts or combine them into one website — a lot of groups do the second, because a shopper does not care which of your lots a truck is parked on.

Am I signing a contract?

No. Month to month. The whole pitch is that this pays for itself in one extra unit turned, and if it does not, you should not be locked in.
