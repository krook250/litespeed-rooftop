Unlimited units

No per-unit pricing, no tier that steps up at 75 cars, no reason to hold inventory back out of the system.

One record per VIN

Year, make, model, trim, mileage, colors, drivetrain, options, title status, cost, pack, recon, price and photos in one place.

Days in stock, counted for you

Every unit carries its real age from the day you acquired it. It does not reset because somebody re-saved the record.

Aging buckets

0–15, 16–30, 31–45, 46–60 and 61+, color-coded everywhere a car appears so you see age without doing math.

How connecting an aggregator actually works, since nobody explains this.

There is no "sign in with CarGurus" button anywhere in this industry, and no vendor has one. Cars.com, Autotrader and CarGurus each provision one registered feed source per dealer account, by hand, through your account rep. So we generate your feed, you tell your rep your inventory now comes from Rooftop, and they switch the source over. It takes days, not minutes, and we do the back-and-forth for you as part of onboarding.

Leads coming back the other way are instant and free: you paste one Rooftop address into the lead-notification field of each account you already have, and they all start arriving in one inbox. That part needs permission from nobody.

Most dealer software holds the current state of a car and quietly overwrites the last one. Lot Walk keeps the whole history: a real event, on a real VIN, with a person and a timestamp on it. That log is what makes the reporting further down possible at all.

The feed

Every card carries a number

No card posts without days in stock, gross, views or dollars on it. That rule is enforced in the code, not by whoever wrote the card.

Comments, reactions and @mentions

Your crew replies on the specific car instead of in a group text nobody can search three weeks later.

Post your own

A composer for the things the system cannot know. Scoped to a rooftop so the other lot is not reading about your Tuesday.

Activity log mode

Two people at the store and no appetite for a social feed? Flip the same stream to a plain timestamped dealer log. No avatars, no reactions, 120 rows a page.

House default, personal override

The owner sets which view the store opens on. Anyone can choose differently for themselves and does not get overwritten when the owner changes their mind.

Switching loses nothing

It is one stream shown two ways, so old comment threads survive a switch in either direction.

Management rails

At-risk units, water units, the recon bay and inbound transfers sit beside the feed in both views, so the list you need is never a menu away.

Who is on the lot today

A staff rail beside the feed, so you know who is around before you go looking for them.

Mute card types Building

Roll-up digests Building

Owner, sales, recon and porter, each on their own account. Nobody shares a password taped to the monitor.

Your data is scoped to you

Every screen and every query is fenced to your dealership at the database layer. Another dealer opening one of your links gets nothing.

It fails closed, not open

A query that forgets to say which dealership it is for returns nothing rather than the next dealer's inventory — and the compiler enforces it, not a code review.

The domain is registered to you

Your name and details go in the ownership record, not ours. If you leave, you take it.

No contract

The part every other feature page leaves out

What ninety-nine dollars buys, and what it cannot.

Everyone in this category advertises "syndication to 400+ sites" and hopes you do not ask the obvious follow-up question. So here is the answer before you ask it. There are three kinds of channel, and only the first one is included in any vendor's monthly price — ours or theirs.

Included, and free to send

  • Your own dealer website
  • Meta catalog & Automotive Inventory Ads
  • Facebook Marketplace ad placement
  • Google Vehicle Ads
  • The long-tail listing sites

Nothing to transmit these costs anybody anything. You pay ad spend on the paid ones and nothing on the rest.

Your contract — we feed it

  • Cars.com
  • Autotrader
  • CarGurus
  • Carfax listings

These are subscriptions you hold directly, commonly $800–$3,000 a month each, and no software company includes them at any price. We will take over the feed into the accounts you already pay for, and we will not pretend ninety-nine dollars bought them.

Not available to anyone

  • Free organic Facebook Marketplace listings

Meta stopped distributing dealer inventory into Marketplace from feeds in September 2021 and removed the vehicles tab from dealer Pages in 2023. Neither came back, and there is no partner program to join. Any vendor selling you "automatic Marketplace posting" today is running a bot against one of your salespeople's personal Facebook accounts, which Facebook restricts. We will not put your people's accounts at risk, and Marketplace ad placement above is the sanctioned way in.

If that reads like we just talked ourselves out of four checkboxes, fair. But a dealer paying CarGurus two thousand a month at two hundred dollars a lead deserves to hear this from a vendor once, and the one time it is going to happen is before you sign up rather than ninety days after.
